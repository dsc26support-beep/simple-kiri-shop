Connect an Android phone

  1. Install the Mwakete SMS app on the phone that has your SIM.
  2. Press Get a code here, then type the code into the app within 10 minutes.
  3. Allow the app to send SMS when Android asks.

Sending

No messages go out in quiet hours (Kiribati time); they wait until the morning. A gap between messages keeps your SIM from being blocked as spam.

People who opted out

They replied STOP or used the opt-out page. They get no promotions or announcements from you. To undo one, change their consent on the Contacts page.

Google Sheets add-on

A key lets the Mwakete add-on read and update your contacts and draft campaigns from a Google Sheet. It cannot send or start a campaign. Keep it secret; remove it if a sheet is shared.
