Find your Messenger link

Customers tap Messenger on your store page to chat with you. For that, Mwakete needs your Facebook profile name — the part after facebook.com/ in your profile’s address. You can paste the whole link; we keep just the name and turn it into a Messenger link (https://m.me/yourname).

On a phone (Facebook app)

  1. Open the Facebook app and tap your profile picture (or Menu ☰ → your name).
  2. On your profile, tap the three dots … next to Edit profile.
  3. Scroll down to Your profile link and tap Copy link.
  4. Come back to Mwakete and paste it into the Facebook Messenger box. That’s it.

The link looks like https://www.facebook.com/your.name, or https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=1000… if you never chose a username — both work.

On a computer

  1. Go to facebook.com and log in.
  2. Click your name or profile picture (top left, or under the menu on the right).
  3. Look at the address bar: facebook.com/your.name. The highlighted part is your profile name.
  4. Copy the whole address (Ctrl+C) and paste it into the Facebook Messenger box on Mwakete.

If you sell from a Facebook Page

  1. Open your Page (switch to it from your profile picture if Facebook asks).
  2. Copy its address, e.g. facebook.com/BongStoreKiribati.
  3. Paste it into the Messenger box. Customers will then message the Page, not your personal account — good if several people answer.

No username on the Page yet? In the Page’s settings, look for Username and pick one. Until then, its profile.php?id=… link works too.

Check your link works

Paste your profile name or link here. We show the Messenger link we would save — tap it to make sure it opens a chat with you (or your Page).

Nothing is saved on this page. Paste the same thing into the Messenger box on Register Store or Settings.

Reaching sellers and customers on Messenger

Shoppers: messaging a seller

  1. Open the seller’s store page on Mwakete.
  2. Tap the Messenger button. It opens a chat with the seller straight away — nothing to look up.
  3. No Facebook? Use Chat with seller on Mwakete, or their phone / WhatsApp on the store page.

Sellers: reaching a customer

  1. Easiest: ask the customer to tap Messenger on your store page and say hello — their message arrives in your Messenger, and you just reply.
  2. Or ask them to send you their profile link (they can follow the “On a phone” steps above), then open it and tap Message.
  3. For an order, their phone number is on the order — call or WhatsApp if Messenger isn’t answered.
Stay safe: share only your profile link. Never send your Facebook password or a login code to anyone — Mwakete will never ask for them.
