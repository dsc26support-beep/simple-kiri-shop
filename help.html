    Frequently asked questions

    Getting started

    Mwakete is an online marketplace where shops, businesses and individuals in Kiribati list products, services and rentals, and shoppers find them and contact them.

    Mwakete does not sell anything itself and never handles your money. When you buy, the agreement is between you and the seller.

    Related

    • Who can use Mwakete?
    • How do I pay?

    Anyone 18 or older can use Mwakete, or a younger person with the agreement of a parent or guardian. You can browse and order without an account.

    Please give real contact details - a seller cannot deliver to a wrong number.

    Safety & Trust

    Message sellers through Mwakete so there is a record. Check the item and price before you pay, meet in a public place for pickups, and be careful with anyone who pushes you to pay quickly or off the usual route.

    The Verified badge means Mwakete has verified that store. It is not a promise about any particular sale.

    How an order works, and what to agree with the seller. 2 min read

    1. Search or browse Categories to find what you need. Cards show where each item is.
    2. Check the store’s delivery icons - truck, ship, air cargo or Pick & Pay.
    3. Add items to your cart. Each store has its own cart.
    4. At checkout, give your name, phone, island and village, and choose a delivery method.
    5. The seller contacts you to confirm the order, payment and delivery. You pay the seller directly.
