Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Mwakete is a marketplace run by MainKT Enterprises. It connects shoppers in Kiribati with local sellers. This page explains, in plain words, what we hold about you, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

You can shop without an account

You do not need to sign in to browse or to place an order. If you order as a guest we still need your name and phone number, because the seller has to be able to reach you to arrange delivery and payment. An account only adds three things: your order history in one place, the ability to leave reviews, and staying signed in on your device.

What we collect

If you create an account

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Whether your email has been confirmed, and when the account was made
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's user ID, and the fact that Google was used. We receive your name, email address and whether Google has verified that address. We never receive your Google password, and we have no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

When you place an order or booking

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, if you give one
  • Your island and village, and the delivery method you chose
  • What you ordered, the prices, and any note you added
  • For bookings, the dates you asked for

When you message a seller

The messages themselves, the name you gave in the chat, and any photo you send in it.

When you leave a review

Your rating, your comment, and the name on your account. Reviews are public — your name is shown next to them on the product page, so use the name you are comfortable being seen.

When you sign in by email code

Your email address and a six-digit code, held only until the code is used or expires.

What stays on your phone and is never sent to us

Some things are stored only in your own browser. We cannot read them, and clearing your browser data deletes them:

  • What is in your cart, for each store
  • The name, phone, email, island and village you last typed at checkout, so you do not have to type them again
  • Your sign-in token, if you are signed in
  • Which stores and products you have looked at, used to show you recently viewed items
  • Where you dragged the chat button, and that you have seen the cookie notice

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run any analytics or tracking service on this site.

Who can see your information

  • A seller sees only their own business. When you order from a store, that seller sees your name, phone number, email if you gave one, your island and village, what you ordered and any note. They cannot see orders you placed with any other store, and they cannot see your account details beyond what that order contains.
  • Other shoppers see only your reviews, with the name on your account.
  • MainKT Enterprises can see the records needed to run the marketplace and to help when something goes wrong.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Services we rely on

Running the site means some of your information passes through other companies:

  • Google — the marketplace's records are held in Google Sheets and the site's backend runs on Google Apps Script. Google Sign-In is used only if you choose it.
  • Cloudinary and Google Drive — store product and shop photos.
  • Resend — sends the emails, such as your sign-in code and order confirmations.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the website itself.

If you tap a seller's WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger button, you leave Mwakete and continue in that app, under that company's own privacy policy. We have no view of what you send there.

How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is open and for as long as we need them for the marketplace's records. Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Staying signed in lasts 60 days on your device, or 12 hours if you tick “This is a shared device” when signing in.

Shared phones

If you sign in on a phone other people use, tick “This is a shared device” so you are signed out sooner, and use Log out when you are finished. Anyone using that phone while you are signed in can see your order history, which includes your phone number and delivery address.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can change your name and phone number yourself on your account page. To see everything we hold about you, correct it, or have your account and its records deleted, email admin@mwakete.com from the address on your account and say what you would like done.

Some order records may be kept after an account is deleted where a seller still needs them for a transaction that already happened.

Children

Mwakete is meant for adults. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
